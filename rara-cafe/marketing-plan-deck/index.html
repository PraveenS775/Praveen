Section · 01

Google Business
Profile Fix List

Free, takes one hour, and is the single highest-leverage thing your friend can do this week.

Do this in one sitting · 60 minutes total

The 1-hour Google Business Profile checklist

  • Sign in to the listing and confirm it's verified/claimed (the "limited view" banner suggests it may not be)5 min
  • Add a website link, even a WhatsApp link (wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX) or Linktree works if there's no site yet3 min
  • Upload 15-20 photos: exterior in daylight, interior seating, 5-6 best dishes, the bar/coffee station, one event-night shot20 min
  • Add the menu (Google's native menu editor, or upload the PDF from the IG offer posts)10 min
  • Confirm hours are accurate for every day, including the special event nights2 min
  • Add the Zomato/Swiggy ordering link under "Order online" if listed there5 min
  • Write a 1-paragraph "From the business" description mentioning cuisine, vibe, and Miryalaguda location5 min
  • Reply to every existing review, including old ones, it signals an active, cared-for business10 min
This is the cheapest, fastest win in the whole plan. A complete profile with photos and a website link converts far more map-views into footfall.

Keep the 4.8★ growing

Turn happy tables into Google reviews, systematically

Table QR code

A small printed card on every table with a QR code linking straight to the Google review page. Costs nothing, takes a customer 20 seconds.

Staff ask script

Train staff to say one line when a table looks happy: "If you enjoyed it, a quick Google review really helps us." Verbal asks convert better than signage alone.

Bill footer line

Print "Loved your visit? Review us on Google" with the QR on the printed bill, the moment they're most likely to act.

Section · 02

30-Day Instagram
Content Calendar

4 posts/week + daily stories. Mixed formats so the grid doesn't feel like a bulletin board.

Before the calendar: the rules behind it

Why this cadence and mix

4 feed posts / week

Enough to stay visible in followers' feeds without burning out the kitchen/photo workflow. One of the 4 is always a Reel.

One consistent template per post type

Offer posts, event posts, and dish posts each get one fixed layout (same fonts/colors as the logo) instead of mixed Canva templates.

Daily stories, no exceptions

A 5-second story of "today's special" or the morning prep keeps the account alive between feed posts, costs almost no effort.

Section · 03

3 Reel Scripts

Built from what's already happening at the cafe, no extra production needed.

Shoot these on a phone, no edit skills required

Three reels using what's already there

A

The Pour (15s)

Hook (0-2s): close-up of iced hazelnut coffee being poured, caramel drizzle going on.
Build (2-10s): slow-mo pour, steam/condensation on the glass, hand placing it on the wood table.
Payoff (10-15s): first sip, satisfied reaction, text overlay "RaRa Cafe, Miryalaguda."

Music: trending chill/lofi audio. Caption: "Slow mornings start here ☕"

B

Kitchen to Table (20s)

Hook (0-3s): sizzling pan or wok shot, steam and motion.
Build (3-15s): quick cuts: plating, garnish, the dish being carried to the table.
Payoff (15-20s): overhead shot of the finished spread, text overlay "Order this combo, link in bio."

Music: upbeat trending audio with a beat-drop on the plating shot. Caption: "Watch this come together 👀"

C

Event Night Recap (15-20s)

Hook (0-2s): crowd shot, lights, energy from the comedy/musical night.
Build (2-14s): quick cuts of performer, crowd reactions, drinks being served.
Payoff (14-20s): text overlay with date/time of the NEXT event + "Buy passes, link in bio."

Music: the actual live audio from the event if usable, otherwise trending upbeat track. Caption: "Last night was loud, in the best way."

Reels get disproportionate reach for small accounts right now. These three formats can be repeated monthly with new dishes/events.
